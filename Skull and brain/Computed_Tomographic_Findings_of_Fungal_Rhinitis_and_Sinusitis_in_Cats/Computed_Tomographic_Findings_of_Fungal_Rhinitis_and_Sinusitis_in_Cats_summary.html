01 · Overview

這篇文章真正要你記住什麼？

Feline fungal sinonasal disease 沒有單一 CT signature；它可以呈現高度侵襲性，並與 neoplasia 明顯重疊。
Clinical problem

Chronic nasal/ocular discharge

CT pattern

Soft tissue + destruction + extension

Main pitfall

Looks neoplastic

Next step

Rhinoscopy + histology/culture/testing

Bottom line:侵襲性骨破壞、鼻甲骨溶解、眼眶延伸與 mass effect 並不等於 neoplasia。Fungal rhinitis/sinusitis 必須保留在重要鑑別診斷中。

Nasal enhancement

06 · Diagnostic overlap

為什麼它容易被誤認為 neoplasia？

Features that can look neoplastic

• Older age group

• Soft tissue mass effect

• Moderate/severe turbinate destruction

• Nasal septum lysis

• Paranasal osteolysis

• Orbital extension

• Nasopharyngeal mass

Features not seen in this fungal series

• Maxillary bone lysis

• Bilateral orbital lamina lysis

• Universal bilateral disease

These absences are descriptive only and should not be used as exclusion criteria because n=10.

Species-specific point:作者指出犬鼻腔 aspergillosis 常見的 cavitated turbinate lysis 與典型 rim-like soft tissue pattern，並未在這個 feline aspergillosis 系列中出現。不要直接把 canine pattern 套用到貓。

08 · Clinical pearls

影像科實戰重點

1. Aggressive ≠ neoplastic.

骨破壞與眼眶延伸可以是真菌感染。

2. Bilateral is common, not mandatory.

8/10 bilateral，但 2/10 是 unilateral right-sided disease。

3. Enhancement is variable.

Peripheral、heterogeneous、homogeneous 都可能出現。

4. Scan beyond the nasal cavity.

Frontal sinus、nasopharynx、bulla 與 regional nodes 都可能受累。

5. Lymph nodes may be diagnostically useful.

5/9 enlarged；某病例 node biopsy 直接證實 Aspergillus hyphae。

6. Cat ≠ dog.

不要直接套用 canine nasal aspergillosis 的 classic CT pattern。

Critical appraisal: n=10、retrospective、multiple fungal organisms、不同掃描器與 slice thickness、沒有 disease-control group。這篇文章最適合用來擴充 differential diagnosis，而不是建立 diagnostic rule-out/rule-in criteria。
